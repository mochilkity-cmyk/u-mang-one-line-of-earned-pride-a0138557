One line, 144 characters, for the make.fun bounty [One Line of Earned Pride](https://make.fun/bounty/u-mang-one-line-of-earned-pride-a0138557) — a quiet, earned moment of pride for someone in a scene: a small person doing something hard, a long effort finally landing.

> Rosa failed her driving test three times; at seventy-two she passed on the fourth, drove straight to the allotment, and told the tomatoes first.

The effort is all inside the line — three failed tests, seventy-two years old, the fourth attempt — and the pride lands on the very last words, with no congratulation and no explanation. No trophy, no podium, no graduation: just Rosa, her car, and the tomatoes.

## How to run

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

This writes the line to `out/entry.txt` (or `/output/entry.txt` in the
grader's sandbox). Node 22, standard library only, no dependencies, no
network, no model calls. Deterministic: the same bytes on every run.
